# Contributing

This document applies only when someone voluntarily submits a change back to
this repository. Apache-2.0 does not require users to open a pull request, ask
for approval, or publish their modifications here. Forks and independent ports
may be developed and distributed separately under the applicable license.

Submit only material that you created yourself or that you have documented
permission to distribute.

Contributions to paths listed in `PORTING_CODE.md` are submitted under the
Apache License, Version 2.0. Contributions to `addons/wmv_video` are submitted
under that component's MIT License unless a file clearly states otherwise.
Submitting a file elsewhere does not automatically assign it a license.

By submitting a contribution, you certify that:

- you wrote the contribution or have the right to submit it under the stated
  license;
- it does not contain source code, scripts, dialogue, media, fonts, or data
  extracted, copied, translated, transcribed, converted, or decompiled from
  WHITE ALBUM2 or another proprietary work;
- any third-party material is clearly identified and its redistribution terms
  are satisfied;
- you understand that the contribution and its history may be distributed
  publicly under the applicable license.

Do not submit original game archives, extracted resources, credentials, signing
keys, build packages containing game data, or generated editor caches. Review
`ASSET_POLICY.md`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and the staged diff before every
push. Maintainers should reject a contribution when its source or redistribution
permission cannot be verified.
